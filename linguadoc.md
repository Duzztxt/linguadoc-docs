# LínguaDoc: the ideal solution for students

## Overview

**LínguaDoc** is a platform designed for students who frequently need to organize, review, and connect academic materials.

With LínguaDoc, students can create reading notes from articles, register essays, store questions from their academic courses, and bring all this content together into a personal knowledge base. This helps students connect information, review content more easily, and make better use of the materials they produce throughout their academic journey.

## Features

LínguaDoc offers features focused on organizing, producing, and reviewing academic content.

### Reading note creation

Students can create reading notes directly on the platform, without needing to use other text-editing tools such as Google Docs or Microsoft Word.

This feature allows students to record important information from articles, books, chapters, or other study materials, keeping all content centralized in one place.

### Automatic ABNT references

After the required reference information is filled in, LínguaDoc automatically generates the reference in ABNT format.

This feature helps students standardize their academic references and reduces the time spent on manual formatting adjustments.

### Information cross-referencing

After students add reading notes, articles, essays, or questions to the platform, LínguaDoc allows them to connect this information within their knowledge base.

This makes it possible to identify connections between topics, authors, courses, and study materials, making the review and research process more effective.

### Question generation

Students can select a PDF uploaded to the platform and have LínguaDoc automatically generate questions based on the document’s content.

This feature can be used to review reading materials, test content comprehension, and create study activities.

## Target audience

LínguaDoc was designed for students who deal with a large volume of reading, writing, and academic material organization.

The platform is especially useful for undergraduate students, graduate students, and early-stage researchers who need to create reading notes, review texts, organize references, and connect different study materials.

It can also be useful for students in courses that require frequent reading and writing, such as Language and Literature, History, Philosophy, Law, Education, and other areas in the Humanities.

## Requirements

To use LínguaDoc, students need internet access, a registered account on the platform, and a compatible device, such as a computer, tablet, or smartphone.

No technical knowledge is required to use the system. The platform was designed to provide a simple and accessible experience for students with different levels of familiarity with digital tools.
